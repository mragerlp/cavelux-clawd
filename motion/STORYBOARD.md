# CAVELUX — Engineered to deliver.

45-second brand film · 1080 × 1920 · 9:16 · 30 fps · 1,350 frames.

## Direction

Clawd carries a clear brief through building, coordinated execution, integration, and delivery. Large editorial typography, crisp pixel geometry, scientific notation, and precise musical punctuation make the process readable. The palette remains restrained until the throughput sequence. This is an illustrative engineering process, not a recording of production systems or evidence of client results, deployment activity, or measured performance.

Reuse Cavelux's existing pixel-eye glyph, glitch wordmark, and eye/swirl logo layers. Character state is explicit: NORMAL has two regular rectangular black eyes and no aura; WORKING has friendly open spiral eyes and a moving green code aura; ULTRACODE has black spiral eyes and an animated flowing rainbow wave. The half-lidded angry expression is retired from active scenes and the asset library. No chains.
