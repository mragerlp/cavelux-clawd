## Direction

A precise, curious little creature becomes a coordinated world. Use large editorial typography, crisp pixel geometry, scientific notation, breathing room, and musical punctuation. The palette remains restrained until the spectrum sequence. The short is a creative process metaphor, not a recording of production systems or a claim about delivered project results.

Character state is explicit: NORMAL has two regular rectangular black eyes and no aura; WORKING has friendly open spiral eyes and a moving green code aura; ULTRACODE has black spiral eyes and an animated flowing rainbow wave. The half-lidded angry expression is retired from active scenes and the asset library. No chains.
